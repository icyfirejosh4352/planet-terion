# planet-terion
2D hack and slash metroidvania.

You play as an astronaut character stuck on Planet Terion. Your goal is to kill the aliens that inhibit the planet and the robots that have malfunctioned. 

# CONTROLS
A and D to move.\
Left Click to Attack. \
F to interact. \
Space to Jump. \
Shift to Dash
